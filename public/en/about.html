    A philanthropic foundation for war victims in Israel bringing hope and renewal

    How It All Began

    Itai Community was founded from the deep understanding that war victims need not only medical treatment, but a holistic and comprehensive support system. Our founders, some of whom are themselves war victims or family members of victims, understood that existing systems do not provide a complete response to the complex needs of people who have experienced war trauma.

    The idea was simple yet revolutionary: to create a philanthropic fund for war victims in Israel that would combine funding for medical and social projects with a warm and supportive community approach. Not just to treat physical wounds, but also to create a healing space where every victim can find their path to renewal.

    Since its establishment, Itai Community has grown and developed into a leading organization in the field of war victim rehabilitation. We operate in collaboration with international foundations, social organizations, companies, corporations and public bodies around the world. These partnerships enable us to bring the most advanced treatments, technologies and knowledge to serve war victims in Israel.

    Our Mission

    Itai Community is committed to providing a holistic and comprehensive response to all war victims in Israel. We work to create innovative social ventures that combine advanced medical treatment, mental and emotional support, occupational integration and a supportive community.

    Our mission is to ensure that every war victim, regardless of their economic or social status, receives the treatment and support they need to return to a full and meaningful life. We believe that true healing comes through a multi-dimensional approach that addresses the whole person.

    Our Vision

    Our vision is to build a society where every war victim receives the response they need, and where the entire community is committed to supporting victims and their families. We strive to create a model of partnership with an international foundation for social projects that will serve as an example to the entire world.

    We envision a future where Itai Community will be an international center of excellence in the field of war victim rehabilitation, a place where innovative treatment methods are developed, groundbreaking research is conducted, and from which experts emerge who influence the field worldwide.

    Our Values

    Compassion and Empathy

    We treat every war victim with compassion, empathy and respect. We understand that every person has gone through difficult experiences and comes to us with unique needs and feelings.

    Community and Belonging

    We believe in the power of community to heal and strengthen. We create spaces where war victims can find support, friendship and a sense of belonging.

    Innovation and Excellence

    We constantly seek new and improved ways to help war victims. We invest in research, development and bringing the most advanced technologies and methods.

    Partnerships and Collaboration

    We believe in the power of partnerships. We collaborate with organizations, foundations, companies and public bodies around the world to bring the best to war victims.

    Equality and Social Justice

    We believe that every war victim is entitled to receive treatment and support, regardless of their economic status, origin or other characteristics. We work for equality and social justice.

    Transparency and Accountability

    We operate with complete transparency and are committed to accountability towards war victims, our donors and partners. We publish annual reports and track the results of our ventures.

    Our Team

    Itai Community consists of a dedicated team of professionals, volunteers and former war victims. Each of our team members brings experience, expertise and deep commitment to our mission.

    Medical and Psychological Team

    Our medical and psychological team includes doctors, psychologists, physiotherapists, occupational therapists and nurses. All have extensive experience in treating trauma victims and specialize in relevant fields. The team undergoes continuous training and participates in international conferences and workshops.

    Social and Community Team

    Our social workers, case managers and community coordinators provide personal accompaniment and ongoing support to every participant. They coordinate between different ventures, assist with forms and rights, and serve as a central point of contact for participants and their families.

    Business Development and Partnerships Team

    Our business development team works on creating new partnerships with international foundations, companies and organizations. They develop new ventures, raise resources and ensure that Itai Community continues to grow and develop.

    Volunteers

    Hundreds of volunteers contribute their time and skills to Itai Community. They accompany participants, organize activities, assist with administrative tasks and contribute in many other ways. Our volunteers are the heart and soul of the community.

    Our International Partnerships

    Partnership with an international foundation for social projects is a central part of our strategy. We collaborate with leading organizations around the world that specialize in trauma healing, war victim rehabilitation and social venture development.

    Access to Global Knowledge

    Our partnerships enable us to access knowledge, research and advanced treatment methods from around the world. We learn from the experience of other organizations and apply the lessons in our work.

    Additional Funding for Initiatives

    Funding medical and social projects requires extensive resources. Our partnerships with international foundations enable us to raise additional funding and expand our ventures.

    Expertise and Consulting

    Our partners provide us with professional consulting, staff training and support in developing new ventures. We gain access to top experts in various fields.

    International Network

    Our partnerships connect us to a global network of organizations, experts and activists in the field of war victim rehabilitation. This network enables us to share knowledge, learn and collaborate on joint projects.

    Success Stories

    Beyond numbers, our real impact is measured by the success stories of the people we serve. We have seen war victims return to work, complete studies, start families and find new meaning in life. We have seen families recover from severe trauma and rebuild their relationships. Every story is testimony to the power of healing and the importance of a supportive community.
